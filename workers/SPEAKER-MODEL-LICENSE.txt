Models are not included in the setup or portable package. Exact official download
URLs, sizes and hashes are locked in speaker-model.json. No model code is vendored
or reimplemented. Downloaded wheels retain their .dist-info license notices.
This app does not identify real-world people: it proposes anonymous voice groups
for subtitle intervals. Character names are entered by the user. Voice groups
and candidate references require review, especially with noisy, short, overlapping
or expressive speech. This model was trained on English VoxCeleb recordings;
performance on other languages and film soundtracks is not established here.
